ATS Resume Checklist

A good ATS-friendly resume should use clear section headings and readable formatting.

  • Skills section
  • Education section
  • Work experience section
  • Projects section
  • Certifications section

Resume Improvement Tips

Before submitting your resume, make sure it is customized to the job description and highlights your most relevant skills.

  • Use keywords from the job description.
  • Avoid unclear section headings.
  • Keep formatting simple and readable.
  • Describe projects and experience clearly.

Current prototype note: Job seeker resume history is not permanently stored in the database. Uploaded and built resume data are processed temporarily for analysis and resume generation.
