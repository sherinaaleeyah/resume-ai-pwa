Resume tips

Mirror the job's keywords

Use skills and tools exactly as the job description names them. It improves ATS matching.

Use standard section headings

Labels like "Skills" and "Experience" are read reliably. Creative headings often aren't.

Keep formatting simple

Avoid tables, text boxes and columns. They can scramble how your resume is parsed.

Show outcomes, not duties

Describe projects and experience with specific, measurable results.

Tailor your summary

Rewrite your professional summary for each role you apply to.
