Best Practices

Resume Improvement Tips

Before submitting, make sure your resume is customized to the job description and highlights your most relevant skills and experience.

  • Use keywords directly from the job description to improve ATS matching.
  • Avoid unclear or creative section headings — use standard labels like Skills and Experience.
  • Keep formatting simple, readable, and free of tables or text boxes.
  • Describe projects and work experience with clear, specific outcomes.
  • Tailor your professional summary to the role you are applying for.

Prototype note: Job seeker resume history is not permanently stored in the database. Uploaded and built resume data are processed temporarily for analysis and resume generation only.
