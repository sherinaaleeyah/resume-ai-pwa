How it works

From job posting to shortlist in three steps

No integrations or training needed. Paste a description, add resumes, and review the results.

  1. 01

    Create a job posting

    Paste your job description. TalentMatch extracts the required skills and lets you tune how they're weighted.

  2. 02

    Upload resumes

    Drop in a batch of PDF resumes. Each one is parsed, analyzed, and scored against the role automatically.

  3. 03

    Review the ranked shortlist

    See candidates ranked by fit, with matched and missing skills, strengths, and a summary for each.

Features

Everything you need to screen fairly and quickly

Built to support recruiter judgement, not replace it.

Bulk resume intake

Upload many PDFs at once and get every candidate parsed, scored, and ranked in a single pass.

Explainable scores

Every score comes with matched skills, missing skills, and strengths, so you can see how it was reached.

Skill gap analysis

Spot which required skills are rare across your applicant pool before you start outreach.

Adjustable weighting

Balance skill match against overall relevance for each role, and re-rank candidates instantly.

Resume feedback

Job seekers get a match score, prioritized suggestions, and an action plan for a target role.

Resume builder

Generate a clean, structured resume with AI assistance and export it as a Word document.
